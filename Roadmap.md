# NikahDigital — Product Roadmap

## Vision
NikahDigital aims to become Indonesia's #1 digital wedding invitation platform by combining SaaS convenience with a deep Indonesian cultural theme library. Fokus produk bukan hanya “buat website undangan”, tetapi memberi pasangan Indonesia template yang terasa lokal, mudah dikustomisasi, mobile-first, dan siap dibagikan dalam hitungan menit.

## v2.0 — SaaS MVP Launch (8 weeks from today)
Target window: 2026-04-15 → 2026-06-10
